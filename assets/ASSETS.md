# RealHero image assets

## 0.2.2 mission header

`images/mission-header.png` is a separate raster asset generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using the user's approved Mission reference (`IMG_3348.jpeg`). Prompt: reconstruct only the photograph — closed charcoal-blue notebook and pen on a dark wooden desk, dark mug at right, two plants on a window sill, sunset city light, black-teal shadows; no laptop, open notebook, text, phone frame, icons or interface. Landscape 1536×1024. Used as the native Mission header, not as a screenshot-based UI.

The existing horizon, portrait, globe, and in-progress background assets are retained. Photo cropping, dark overlays, orbit geometry, glow, and all controls are rendered separately in Flutter.

## Today landscape (0.2.2)
`images/horizon-approved.png` was generated with the built-in image tool using the approved Today reference IMG_3350. Prompt: dark teal night-sky upper space, warm sunset over sea and mountain islands, a small full-body hooded traveler on rocks on the right. No text or controls are baked into the asset. The original horizon asset remains available.
